# TCP Maimon Scan

Here’s a **concise markdown summary** of the **Maimon Scan** as described:

---

## **🔎 TCP Maimon Scan (Nmap -sM)**

### **📜 Origin**

- Described by **Uriel Maimon** in **1996**
- Designed to **bypass some firewall rules and detect open ports** on certain systems (especially **BSD-derived**)

---

### **⚙️ Scan Characteristics**

- **Flags Set:** FIN + ACK
- **Command:**

```
sudo nmap -sM TARGET_IP
```

---

### **🧠 Scan Logic**

- **Closed ports:** Send back an **RST**
- **Open ports (on vulnerable systems):** **Drop** the packet (no response)

This allows Nmap to **infer open|filtered** status when **no RST is received**, but **only works on specific systems**.

---

### **📉 Effectiveness**

- 🔹 **Modern systems:** Usually respond with RST for *both open and closed ports*, making the scan **ineffective**
- 🔹 **Limited use cases:** Can help evade detection or firewalls on **legacy or poorly configured systems**
- 🔹 **Primarily educational** to understand scanning mechanisms and bypass logic
